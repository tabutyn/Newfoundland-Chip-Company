## Budget provenance and arithmetic

- Startup total C$2–5m = staff C$1–2.4m + fit-out C$0.15–0.4m + diagnostic equipment C$0.2–0.65m + overhead C$0.15–0.35m + working capital C$0.2–0.5m + contingency C$0.3–0.7m.
- Startup assumes 18–24 months, 5–8 staggered hires, leased premises and no full cleanroom production line.
- R&D platform C$8–25m and specialty production platform C$30–100m+ are original stage-level estimates with low confidence. They require staffing plans, specifications, quotations and qualification schedules before financing.
- Stepper feasibility C$2–10m funds analysis and demonstrators only. No build-ready total for a commercial stepper has been established. The hundreds-of-millions-to-billions scale statement is an explicit low-confidence inference from engineering scope and incumbent scale, not a sourced procurement price.
- Illustrative unit contribution: C$500k price − C$300k direct build cost − C$50k installation/warranty = C$150k; C$3m fixed cost / C$150k = 20 annual tools. C$3m / C$100k = 30 tools if contribution falls. No sales forecast or profitability claim follows.

5. Scope field service geography, travel cost, contamination handling, liability coverage and required installation approvals.
