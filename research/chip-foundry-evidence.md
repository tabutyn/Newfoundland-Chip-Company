## Proposed first-financing ranges and arithmetic

- Chip Company C$1.5–4.0M: payroll 0.85–2.10; capital 0.08–0.25; EDA/cloud 0.04–0.15; fabrication/packaging/test 0.15–0.50; commercial/admin 0.08–0.20; working capital 0.10–0.30; contingency 0.20–0.50. Exact endpoints sum 1.50 and 4.00.
- Foundry startup C$5–15M: fit-out 0.70–2.00; tools 0.90–3.00; payroll/training 0.90–3.00; outside processing/consumables 0.40–1.20; commercial/review 0.20–0.50; working capital 0.70–1.80; contingency 1.20–3.50. Exact endpoints sum 5.00 and 15.00.
- These are gross cash uses. No subsidy or projected revenue is netted off. Founder compensation and ramped hiring must be rebuilt from quotations; no local salary survey is claimed.
- Foundry phase envelopes: limited pilot C$50–200M; specialty commercial 150/200mm C$1–3B; advanced DRAM strategic option C$15–40B+, not baseline. Whole-stage envelopes are not automatically incremental; asset reuse has to be modeled.
- Chip expansion product phase C$5–20M additional; multiple products/CPU SoC phase C$25–100M+ additional; discrete DRAM/high-end CPU is unbudgeted.

## Calculation checks

- Service example: 900 h × C$90 + C$15,000 = C$96,000; C$150,000 revenue minus cost = C$54,000 contribution, 36%.
- ASIC example: 100,000 × (C$12 − C$4) = C$800,000; C$5,000,000 / C$8 = 625,000 units before fixed cost/finance.
- Lab example: 3 × 1,500 h × C$250 = C$1,125,000, below illustrative C$2M annual costs.
- Wafer example: 5,000 × 12 × 0.60 × 0.90 = 32,400; C$60M / 32,400 = C$1,851.85 fixed cash cost/accepted wafer.
- At 85% utilization: 5,000 × 12 × 0.85 × 0.90 = 45,900; C$60M / 45,900 = C$1,307.19.
