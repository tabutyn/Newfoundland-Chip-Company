## First validation priorities

Customer demand and experienced leadership come first for the service/design businesses. Grey River resource qualification is a parallel research branch and must not prevent those businesses from testing their own markets. Firm process rights, demand, utilities and independently estimated costs become hard requirements before a production facility.

Close assumptions with evidence, a date and a reviewer. Preserve failure results and rejected options. Mark claims as unknown when confidentiality prevents public verification; do not silently convert a private assertion into a public fact.
