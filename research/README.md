The [capital model](../finance/README.md) uses original assumptions, not conversions or regressions fitted to these comparators. Such sparse, dissimilar examples cannot establish a bankable local cost.

## How to add or revise a claim

Use the [source-record template](../templates/source-record.md). Record the precise claim supported, scope, retrieval date and whether the full source was inspected. Link directly to the primary source and keep an explicit uncertainty or limitation. If a source changes, preserve the previous claim in history and explain the revision.

Use [assumptions.md](assumptions.md) for unsolved questions. A proposal may remain useful while an assumption is unverified, but financing or construction should not rely on that assumption as if it were established.
