## Original estimate basis and cross-file consistency

All proposed ranges are low-confidence screening assumptions in constant CAD2026. Their basis is scoped headcount/runway for W1 and order-of-magnitude industrial scoping with external scale checks for W2–W4. No currency conversion of foreign comparators was performed. No revenue, financing, subsidy, customer, mineral resource or price is assumed as verified.

- W0 C$0.1–0.3M first 3–6 months; nested within W1 if funded together.
- W1 C$2–5M financing for a 24-month small technical distribution/outsourced-services operation; first paid shipments 6–18 months; four to eight staff by end of period; equipment/fit-out C$0.4–1.0M, team/lab/overhead C$1.0–2.4M, inventory/receivables C$0.3–0.7M, contingency C$0.3–0.9M.
- W2 C$15–50M; qualified 100/150 mm pilot finishing/reclaim in 2–5 years from stage start, using imported substrate material.
- W3 C$100–500M; specialty prime wafers with crystal growth, 5–10 years from programme kickoff.
- W4 C$1–5B+; full upstream integrated option through partners/JVs, 10–20+ years; capacity unspecified and needs feasibility, not construction funding guidance.
- W1 illustrative unit case: 200 − 125 − 35 − 10 = C$30 contribution; 1,200,000 / 30 = 40,000 wafers/year fixed cash cost break-even; debt, tax, depreciation and replacement capex excluded. Not market-derived.

No independence or guaranteed technical progression between W2 and W3 is assumed. Stage budgets must not simply be summed; consolidated project planning must resolve reused assets and added capacity.
