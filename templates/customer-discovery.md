## Questions

1. What task or product is the buyer responsible for? What happens when it fails?
2. How do they solve it now, and what do they spend or lose? Separate documented numbers from estimates.
3. Which specification, qualification, delivery and support requirements are mandatory?
4. Who selects the supplier, controls the budget and signs the purchase?
5. What would justify changing suppliers? What would prevent a purchase?
6. What quantity, frequency, lead time and payment terms would a real order require?
7. Would they pay for a bounded diagnostic, engineering study or pilot? What must it deliver?
8. What next action did they agree to, if any?
