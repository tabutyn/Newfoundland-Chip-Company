## Interpretation

Explain the distinction between the source's claim and any inference this project makes. Link the company plan, assumption or decision affected. Record contradictory evidence when relevant.

For costs, retain original currency, price year, location, capacity, schedule and whether the figure is an announced investment, grant, equipment price or full project budget. State any conversion method separately.

For mineral claims, distinguish occurrence, exploration result, resource, reserve and qualified customer material. For facility listings, distinguish published inventory from verified operational condition and secured access.

Link to third-party material instead of copying full articles, restricted reports, standards or supplier manuals. An inaccessible or confidential source may support a private decision but must not be described as publicly verified.
