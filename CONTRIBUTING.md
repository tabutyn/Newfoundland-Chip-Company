# Contributing

The purpose of this project is to make useful, freely implementable plans for an industry in Newfoundland. Contributions should help another person make an informed decision or complete a real next step.

## What to contribute

- Evidence from customers, industrial operators, technical literature, official sources, and authorized supplier quotes.
- Corrections to assumptions, process boundaries, capital scope, lead times, workforce needs, or commercial logic.
- Small reproducible models with units and clearly labelled input assumptions.
- Practical training proposals, candidate products, independent reviews, and completed decision records.

Use the [initial backlog](docs/backlog.md). Describe the decision your contribution changes, what evidence supports it, and what remains unknown. Submit one coherent change at a time.

## Evidence rules

1. Distinguish **verified fact**, **planning assumption**, **target**, **hypothesis**, and **unresolved question**. A target is never evidence that it has been achieved.
2. Cite primary sources with publisher, title, publication/update date when known, access date, URL, and the precise claim supported. Use the [source template](templates/source-record.md).
3. Preserve the currency, year, geography, capacity, and project scope of external cost benchmarks. Do not silently convert a press release into a supplier quote.
4. Include both successful and failed experiments when you have permission to publish them. Summarize confidential evidence only with the owner's permission and mark public verification limits.
5. Keep original planning estimates separate from reported data. Change [financial assumptions](finance/assumptions.json) and affected summaries together.
6. Do not imply institutional endorsement, resource ownership, regulatory approval, or a business relationship without verifiable authorization.

## Review and rights

Original contributions are offered under the repository's MIT License. Retain third-party notices and do not upload material you cannot license: confidential PDKs, purchased standards, restricted CAD libraries, NDA-bound customer requirements, or copied supplier manuals. Link to public sources rather than copying them wholesale.

A proposed plan can be merged without being certified as an engineering design. Claims of production readiness, qualified yields, safety performance, or bankable economics require the evidence and appropriately qualified review described in [governance](docs/governance.md).

The MIT License does not require implementers to contribute changes back. Returning improvements, results, and lessons is welcome. Contributions and maintainership do not convey equity in any business someone later creates.
