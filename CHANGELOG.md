# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-21

- Established an MIT-licensed, freely implementable Newfoundland computer-industry vision with four proposed companies.
- Added basic company plans with staged capital, initial customer hypotheses, revenue paths, timelines and investment gates.
- Separated computer/module assembly, design, prototype fabrication, qualified specialty manufacturing and advanced DRAM.
- Recorded Grey River material suitability as an unresolved geological and commercial question.
- Added a 2026–2056 roadmap, local feasibility work, a first-90-days program, evidence registers and a 20-task backlog.
- Included editable financial assumptions and a Python capital/inflation calculator.
- Added contribution, issue, decision and customer-discovery templates.

This release contains planning documents and a calculator. It contains no production-ready chip layout, fabrication process, machine design, verified mineral resource, secured financing or signed customer contract.
